3 · Pick a device and look inside it

Connects and lists the services and characteristics the device offers (the Bluetooth “menu” an app talks to). Reads the harmless ones (name, model, battery). Nothing is written.

    4 · Shearwater handshake (read-only)

    Chooses a Shearwater computer and asks it three questions: serial number, firmware and model. These are the same requests every download starts with. If this works, the download will work.

    5 · Report
