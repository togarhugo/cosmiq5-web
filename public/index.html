Shearwater Peregrine

The Peregrine hands its dives to the Shearwater Cloud app. Export them from there and choose the file here:

  1. Download your dives from the Peregrine into Shearwater Cloud, as you normally do.
  2. In Shearwater Cloud, export the database: on a phone open Profile and tap Export (the database .db or the account export, a .zip); on a computer use File › Export › Export Whole Database. Save it to your phone or to Files / Downloads. The menu names differ between app versions.
  3. Drag the file into the box below (or tap the box to choose it). A UDDF export works too.

Dives you have already imported are skipped, so you can import a newer export again and again. The Peregrine keeps its own log: its dives are listed separately from the Cosmiq+. A dive that both computers recorded is counted once in the Summary and the regulator counter, using the Cosmiq+ record; it is marked “also on Cosmiq+” here.

Bluetooth download (experimental)

Reads the Peregrine directly, without Shearwater Cloud. It had not worked on a real Peregrine when this was released: if your phone does not list the Peregrine, use the file above.

On the Peregrine open its Bluetooth menu, then tap the button and choose it in the list.

Dive Managerv?
  • iPhone: Safari cannot use Bluetooth. Open this page in the Bluefy browser to connect. Android: use Chrome.
  • Sync: turn the dive computer on and keep it close. Only new dives are downloaded, and each one is saved as it arrives.
  • Depth profiles: the dive computer keeps profiles for about the last 128 dives and overwrites older ones, so sync after each trip.
  • Your data (dives, picture, certifications, regulator) is stored in your private account and is only visible to you.
