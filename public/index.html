Shearwater Peregrine

Download over Bluetooth

  1. Use Bluefy on an iPhone, or Chrome on Android or a computer (Safari cannot do Bluetooth).
  2. On the dive computer switch Bluetooth on in its menu, and close Shearwater Cloud everywhere (a computer talks to one app at a time).
  3. Tap the button and choose your dive computer in the list. Only new dives are downloaded, each saved as it arrives.
Switch Bluetooth on in the computer's menu, then tap the button and choose it in the list.
Download log

Every Bluetooth attempt on this device (a download, a test): what was chosen, each step with its time, who answered, the result and the last bytes exchanged. Use it to see where a failed attempt stopped.

    Or import a file from Shearwater Cloud

    The Peregrine hands its dives to the Shearwater Cloud app. Export them from there and choose the file here:

    1. Download your dives from the Peregrine into Shearwater Cloud, as you normally do.
    2. In Shearwater Cloud, export the database: on a phone open Profile and tap Export (the database .db or the account export, a .zip); on a computer use File › Export › Export Whole Database. Save it to your phone or to Files / Downloads. The menu names differ between app versions.
    3. Drag the file into the box below (or tap the box to choose it). A UDDF export works too.

    Garmin does not let other apps read your dives directly, so you bring the dive files over. Dives are in FIT files (.fit), alone or inside a ZIP:

    1. One dive: on the Garmin Connect website, open the dive, choose the gear menu › Export Original. You get a ZIP with the .fit inside. On an iPhone use Safari, open connect.garmin.com, tap aA › Request Desktop Website (the Garmin Connect app has no export); the ZIP is saved in Files and you choose it here.
    2. All your dives: in your Garmin account go to Data Management › Export Your Data. Garmin emails you a big ZIP (it can take some hours or days). Drop that ZIP here: runs, sleep and other activities in it are skipped.
    3. From the watch: connect it to a computer with its cable and copy the files from Garmin/Activity. The Descent Mk1 shows up as a drive; the Mk2, Mk2i and Mk3 do not on a Mac, so use the free OpenMTP or Android File Transfer app there (Windows copies them directly).
    4. Drag the file or ZIP into the box below (or tap the box to choose it).

    Dives you have already imported are skipped, so you can import a newer export again and again. The Peregrine keeps its own log: its dives are listed separately from the Cosmiq+. A dive that both computers recorded is counted once in the Summary and the regulator counter, using the Cosmiq+ record; it is marked “also on Cosmiq+” here.

    Dives you have already imported are skipped, so you can import a newer export again and again. The Garmin keeps its own log, listed separately. A dive that the Cosmiq+ (or Peregrine) also recorded is counted once in the Summary and the regulator counter, using the first computer's record.       API access

      Let another tool (a dashboard, a script, a spreadsheet) read your dive log. A key can only read dives: it cannot change or delete anything and it cannot open this app.

      How to use it

      Base address
      Send your key in a header (never in the web address)
      Authorization: Bearer dm_…
      Endpoints (read only, GET)
      /pingCheck that your key works.
      /summaryNumber of dives and dive time per computer, scuba and freedive.
      /divesYour dives, newest first, 100 at a time.
      /dives/{id}One dive with its depth samples.
      Options, example and notes
      Options for /dives
      limit1 to 500 (default 100; at most 10 with include).
      cursorThe nextCursor of the previous page: asks for the next page.
      orderdesc (default) or asc.
      from, toDays, like 2025-03-14, both included.
      devicecosmiq, peregrine or garmin.
      kindscuba (includes gauge mode) or freedive.
      locationA place name, any capital letters.
      uploadedSinceOnly dives added after this time, like 2025-04-01T10:00:00Z: to fetch just what is new.
      includesamples adds the depth profile; raw also the device bytes (Cosmiq+).
